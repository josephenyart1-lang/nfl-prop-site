Read this before believing any of it

These are model predictions, not advice, and the model has not been shown to beat a sportsbook. Measured against unseen seasons it is a little better than guessing a player's recent average and no more. Its typical miss is around 7.6 pass attempts, 2.4 targets and 5 carries, against lines that sit near 33, 5 and 12.

It also cannot see an injury, a depth-chart change or a snap count. In the week this was first published it had a running back at 6.3 carries whose line was 13.5, because it had not noticed he had become the lead back. Where the model disagrees loudly with a line, the model is usually the one that is wrong.

Nothing here is a recommendation to bet. If you do, risk only what you can lose.
